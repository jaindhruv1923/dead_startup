## 🚀 Key Modules & Pages

1. **🛒 The Asset Vault (`#marketplace`)**:
   - Filter by categories: E-Commerce, AI & Logistics, Creator Economy, HealthTech, PropTech, DevTools.
   - Granular Asset Stripping mode: buy just the code, just the Figma kit, just the domain, or the whole bundle.
   - Search in **Traditional Mode** (keywords) or **AI Copilot Mode** (natural-language prompts).
   - Instant persona buttons: *Riya's Pick: Fashion Store ($1,250)*, *Micro-Fulfillment AI*, *Music Stems*, etc.

2. **📋 Deep Startup Autopsy & Handover Dossier (`#startup/:id`)**:
   - Tombstone hero banner with capital burned, shutdown date, and raw quote from the former CTO.
   - **Staff Handover Dossier**: System architecture overview, one-command terminal boot runbook, `.env` checklist, and CTO transition walkthrough hours.
   - **Code Health Radar**: Automated test coverage percentage, CI/CD health score, lines of code, and IP clean warranty.
   - **Founder Post-Mortem**: Raw story of what worked, the fatal mistake, and advice for the buyer.
   - **AI 2026 Pivot Ideas**: 3 actionable pivot concepts for each dead codebase.
   - **Interactive Granular Stripper Checkout**: Toggle individual assets with live total price calculation.

3. **📦 Founder & Staff Handover Studio (`#studio`)**:
   - 5-step wizard for founders, CTOs, and managerial staff:
     - *Step 1*: Death Certificate, demographics, and **🕶️ Stealth Mode Sale** toggle.
     - *Step 2*: Granular Asset Stripping catalog and individual pricing.
     - *Step 3*: Managerial Handover Pack (Architecture, runbook, .env templates, CTO onboarding availability).
     - *Step 4*: Candid Post-Mortem writeup.
     - *Step 5*: Automated CI/CD health verification and instant publication to the marketplace.

4. **🔒 The Revival Handover & Escrow Room (`#escrow`)**:
   - Full implementation of **Slide 16 flowchart**:
     - *Stage 1*: Escrow Multi-Sig Vault Lock
     - *Stage 2*: Digital IP Assignment Contract Deed (view, sign, or print)
     - *Stage 3*: Credential Vault Unlock (GitHub repo invite, domain auth code, Figma links)
     - *Stage 4*: 48-Hour Inspection Sandbox with interactive verification checklist
     - *Stage 5*: Payout Settlement & Fund Release to Founder (minus 10% fee)
   - Real-time cryptographic protocol audit log terminal.

5. **🧮 AI Dead-Asset Valuator (`#valuation`)**:
   - 6 interactive sliders: Engineering months invested, developer headcount, lines of code, test coverage, Figma screens, and waitlist leads.
   - Quality toggles: Dockerized boot, premium domain, CTO walkthrough hours.
   - Real-time calculations: Replacement cost, fair market liquidation price, buyer savings percentage, DeadStartup fee, and recommended asset split.

6. **🤖 Traditional vs AI-Powered Comparison (`#compare`)**:
   - Direct interactive representation of the **Research Presentation**.
   - Comparative matrix across 7 dimensions (Functionality, UX, Personalisation, Efficiency, Accessibility, Problem-solving, Cost).
   - **Riya's Real Persona Journey Simulator**: Compares 42 hours and $1,600+ of manual searching against 3 minutes and $1,250 on DeadStartup.

7. **📊 Deck & TAM Opportunity Dashboard (`#deck`)**:
   - **Slide 6 TAM/SAM/SOM Pyramid** with interactive currency switcher (**₹ INR** vs **$ USD**).
   - **Slide 5 Buyer Utility Matrix**: 6x6 interactive grid across purchase, delivery, use, supplements, maintenance, and disposal.
   - **Slide 10 & 11 Competitor Matrix**: DeadStartup vs Flippa vs Acquire.com.
   - **Slides 23-25 Interactive 10-Year Financial Forecast Chart**:
     - *Revenue vs Expenses*
     - *Cumulative Profit / Cash Flow*
     - *Annual EBITDA Net Profit*

8. **📜 Post-Mortem Chronicles (`#post-mortems`) & 🔥 Roast & Pivot Board (`#roast`)**:
   - Rich library of founder failure autopsies with upvoting.
   - Community forum where founders post dead or struggling ideas for roasting and AI pivot suggestions.
